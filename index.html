Private software research project

Read-only market research, with verifiable evidence.

ItemEdge is a personally operated research system designed to study pricing, liquidity, and market behavior of transferable virtual game items. Its purpose is to collect observations, assess data quality, and test whether an economic opportunity exists after real-world costs and constraints.

Research-onlyOperator-controlledNo automatic trading

Market observation

Collect and analyze permitted market listings, historical signals, and supporting metadata from selected marketplaces. Access to each source depends on its terms and authorization.

Evidence preservation

Prepare research archives with metadata and integrity checks so observations and derived findings can be reviewed or reproduced.

Google Drive integration

The operator uses an OAuth-authorized desktop workflow to store, retrieve, and verify research archive files in designated Google Drive folders.

Controlled operation

The project is under development. Its collection, retention, recovery, and storage controls are tested independently before continuous unattended operation.

Why this site exists

This informational site explains the ItemEdge desktop research project and its Google OAuth consent configuration. This website itself does not request Google sign-in, access visitors' Google Drive accounts, collect their files, or provide a trading service.

Google Drive access is authorized by the project operator for storing and verifying their own research archives. See the Privacy Policy for the access scope, data handling and revocation options.

ItemEdge is not an investment service or trading platform. It does not execute marketplace transactions as part of this research setup.

Project contact: GitHub profile. Do not send passwords, API keys, tokens or confidential information through public GitHub messages.
