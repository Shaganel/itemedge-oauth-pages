ItemEdge · Google OAuth disclosure

Privacy Policy

Effective: October 16, 2026

ItemEdge is a privately operated, read-only virtual-item market research project. This policy describes how its operator-controlled desktop components are intended to use Google Drive and how this informational website handles visitor information.

1. Google Drive data and permissions

The desktop archiving workflow uses Google OAuth to access the Google Drive account explicitly authorized by its operator. The configured OAuth permission may grant broad access to files and folders in that account. The intended application workflow limits file operations to separately selected research archive and restore-test folders; the OAuth permission itself is not a folder-level security boundary.

Depending on the operation, ItemEdge may list folder and file metadata, create and upload research archives, read back or download files for integrity or restore testing, and access the files it manages. It is not intended to inspect unrelated personal Drive files as part of its normal workflow.

2. Purpose of use

Google Drive data are used solely to store, verify, retrieve, and restore research evidence and related file metadata. The application does not use Google user data for advertising, marketing profiles, or sale to third parties. No Google user data is used to train general-purpose AI models through this application.

3. Storage and retention

Research data may exist in local PostgreSQL storage, temporary archives, and operator-designated folders in Google Drive. OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored in a private local configuration under the operator's control for unattended authorized access. The operator decides which research data are retained or deleted. The software's archive, verification, and retention features are still subject to testing and operator approval; cloud storage or deletion is not guaranteed.

4. Sharing and external services

ItemEdge is intended for the operator's own research. The project does not provide a public sharing feature for Google Drive files and does not sell or broker the account's contents. Data are processed through Google Drive APIs when the operator authorizes those operations. Google's own handling of Drive data is governed by Google's terms and privacy notices.

5. Security

The intended workflow uses OAuth authorization, private local credential storage, integrity verification, and restrictive folder selection. These measures do not eliminate all risks; in particular, an OAuth access grant may be wider than the application's configured destination folders. Only the operator should authorize the application on an account whose permissions and data they understand.

6. Your choices and deletion

The operator can disconnect or revoke ItemEdge's Google authorization using Google Account connections. Revoking access prevents further authorized API activity but does not automatically erase research files already stored in Google Drive. Those files can be reviewed and deleted by the account owner in Drive. Local archives and credentials can be managed on the operator's computer.

7. This website

This static informational website has no account registration, Google sign-in form, analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or application-side cookies. The hosting service may process technical request information under its own policies. Do not include secrets or private data in public GitHub issues or messages.

8. Contact and changes

For project-related privacy questions, contact the operator through the project owner's GitHub profile. The policy may be updated when the research workflow changes; any revised policy will appear on this page.

ItemEdge's Google Drive OAuth authorization is for an operator-controlled research workflow, not a request for general visitors to grant access to their Google accounts.
